Expense Tracker: Money Manager

A simple and convenient way to manage your income, expenses, categories, receipts, and personal financial records.

About the App

Expense Tracker: Money Manager is an Android application developed by Mahant Krupa Technologies to help users organize and manage their personal financial records.

The application is designed to make everyday expense and income management simple, convenient, and easy to understand.

Key Features

Income Management

Record and manage your income transactions in an organized way.

Expense Tracking

Track your daily expenses and organize them using categories.

Categories

Manage expense and income categories according to your needs.

Receipt Images

Optionally attach receipt images to supported transactions for easier record keeping.

Reports & Charts

View your financial information through useful summaries, reports, and visual charts.

Google Drive Backup

Optionally back up and restore supported application data using your Google Drive account.

Privacy & Data

Your financial records are primarily stored locally on your device. The application does not intentionally use your financial records for advertising personalization.

Optional services such as Google Sign-In, Google Drive Backup, Firebase services, and Google AdMob may process information as described in our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Google Drive Backup

Google Drive Backup is an optional feature. If you choose to use it, the application may upload supported backup data to your Google Drive account and access that backup when you choose to restore your data.

Your Google Drive data remains associated with your Google account and is subject to Google's applicable policies and settings.

Advertising

The application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on the application configuration, advertisements may include banner, native, interstitial, and app open advertisements.

Advertising and privacy practices are described in detail in our Privacy Policy.
